How to grow tomatoes on a balcony

6 min read · Updated in May

You don’t need a garden to grow tomatoes: a sunny balcony and a little patience are enough.

Choose cherry tomatoes, which are easy to grow. The plant needs a pot of at least twenty liters, because its roots take up a lot of space. Before you buy one, see our guide: How to choose a pot.

Water and light

Water in the morning, straight onto the soil and not onto the leaves. On hot days, check the soil every evening: if the top two centimeters are dry, it is time to water.

Tomatoes need a lot of sun: at least six hours a day. If your balcony faces north, choose leafy plants instead, such as lettuce or herbs.

What you need

  • A twenty-liter pot with holes in the bottom.
  • Vegetable soil and a little compost.
  • A bamboo cane to tie the plant to.
  • Organic fertilizer every two weeks.

When the fruits are red and slightly soft, cut them off with scissors. Tomatoes ripen better on the plant than in the kitchen.
